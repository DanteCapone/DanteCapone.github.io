Research

I study how biological communities respond to gradients, disturbance, and the tools used to observe them — integrating field ecology, molecular methods, experiments, and quantitative analysis across marine and freshwater systems.

Aquatic communities across gradients and disturbances

I investigate how zooplankton and phytoplankton communities vary across environmental gradients in the California Current Ecosystem and how they respond to episodic perturbations. This work connects cross-shore structure, water-mass context, and biodiversity patterns using molecular, imaging, and environmental datasets.

Projects: zooplankton biodiversity across the California Current · trait-based and size-structured community analysis

Wildfire impacts on aquatic ecosystems

Wildfire is an increasingly important disturbance in coastal and inland aquatic systems. I study how wildfire-derived material influences phytoplankton composition, nutrient dynamics, and trophic interactions using long-term observations, time-series analysis, and ash-addition experiments in marine and freshwater settings.

Projects: phytoplankton change during the 2020 California fire season · wildfire ash mesocosm and dilution experiments

Molecular, experimental, and quantitative approaches

Ecological inference from metabarcoding data depends on careful attention to methodological bias. My work evaluates how PCR conditions influence inferred community composition and applies Bayesian compositional modeling to improve interpretation. I also develop reproducible analytical workflows for complex community datasets.

Projects: PCR bias correction in marine metabarcoding · Bayesian multinomial logistic-normal modeling

Observation and scientific communication

I am interested in how plankton are made visible through different scientific technologies and how those choices shape ecological understanding. This includes work on scientific illustration, visual communication, and interdisciplinary approaches to aquatic science. See also the Art page.

Projects: visualizing plankton and oceanographic knowledge · scientific illustration and conceptual diagrams
