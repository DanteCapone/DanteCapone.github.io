I study how plankton communities respond to environmental gradients, disturbance, and changing observation technologies. My research integrates field oceanography, molecular ecology, imaging, and quantitative analysis to examine how marine communities are structured across the California Current Ecosystem and how they respond to extreme events such as wildfire.

Research Themes

Plankton ecology across gradients

I examine how plankton communities vary across environmental gradients in the California Current Ecosystem, with an emphasis on cross-shore structure, water-mass context, and ecological reorganization.

Molecular and quantitative approaches

My research uses DNA metabarcoding, Bayesian modeling, trait-based analysis, and reproducible workflows to improve ecological inference from complex community data.

Wildfire and coastal ocean change

I investigate how wildfire influences coastal plankton through observational analyses and experiments, with a focus on phytoplankton shifts, nutrient pathways, and disturbance ecology.

Scientific communication and interdisciplinary ocean science

I am interested in how marine ecosystems are measured, visualized, and communicated, and in how observation technologies shape ecological knowledge.
