Dante Capone

Biological Oceanographer and Aquatic Ecologist

Scripps Institution of Oceanography · UC San Diego

I study how wildfire, environmental gradients, and observation systems shape plankton and microbial communities across aquatic ecosystems. My work integrates field ecology, experiments, molecular methods, imaging, and quantitative analysis across marine and freshwater systems.

Research

Aquatic communities across gradients

How zooplankton and phytoplankton communities vary across coastal-offshore gradients, water masses, and productivity regimes.

Wildfire and disturbance ecology

How wildfire-derived material and altered conditions influence plankton composition, nutrients, and trophic interactions in coastal and freshwater systems.

Molecular methods and scientific communication

Improving ecological inference from metabarcoding data, and exploring how observation systems and visualization shape scientific knowledge.

Currently developing mesocosm and experimental work in freshwater and coastal systems, with ongoing manuscript development on wildfire-plankton interactions, zooplankton biodiversity, and metabarcoding methodology.
