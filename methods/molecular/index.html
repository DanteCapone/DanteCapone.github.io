Pipeline Overview

1
Sample Collection

Sterivex filtration (0.2 µm), RNAlater preservation, flash-freeze –80°C

2
DNA Extraction

DNeasy PowerWater Sterivex Kit; Qubit + NanoDrop QC

3
16S PCR Amplification

515F/806R primers; V4 region; triplicate reactions

4
Library Prep & Sequencing

Nextera XT dual indexing; Illumina MiSeq 2×250 bp

5
Bioinformatics

QIIME2 + DADA2 denoising; SILVA 138 taxonomy; R (phyloseq, vegan)
